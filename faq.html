Frequently Asked Questions

What is Crysanto?

Crysanto is a digital investment platform that allows you to invest crypto and earn potential returns.

How do I start investing?

Register, deposit funds (minimum $100), choose your plan, and start.

Do I need prior experience?

No, the platform is designed to be easy for everyone.

What is the minimum deposit?

The minimum deposit is $100.

What deposit methods are accepted?

We accept USDT (TRC-20 & BEP-20), ETH, and BNB. More currencies may be added over time.

How long does deposit confirmation take?

Deposits are reviewed and added to your account within 5-30 minutes after network confirmation.

What is the minimum withdrawal?

The minimum withdrawal is $50.

How long does withdrawal take?

Withdrawal requests are processed within 24 hours maximum.

Can I withdraw anytime?

Yes, you can submit a withdrawal request anytime your balance exceeds the minimum.

Is the investment safe?

We use SSL/HTTPS encryption and identity verification (KYC) to help protect accounts.

What is KYC?

KYC is identity verification, a security measure to protect users and platform integrity.

Is my data protected?

We do not share your data with third parties.
